Hofstadter's MIU System 
$Hypatia Sifr $:
$Lingua MIU $:

  $Alphabetum M I U x y $.
  $Lexicon M I U $.
  $Mutabilia x y $.

  $Principium MI $: MI $.
  $Regula I   $: xI    $- xIU $.
  $Regula M   $: Mx    $- Mxx $.
  $Regula III $: xIIIy $- xUy $.
  $Regula UU  $: xUUy  $- xy  $.

  $Productum MIU $: MIU $ex
    $Probatione
      $Assumo    MI  $per $Principium MI $.
      $Consequor MIU $ex  $Regula I
                     $cum x $= M $.
    $.
  $.

$Hypatia Sifr $:
$Lingua SKI $:

  $Alphabetum  S K I x y z $.
  $Intervalla \sp \th $.
  $Lexicon S K I $.
  $Mutabilia x y z $.

  $Regula I $: I x     $- x         $.
  $Regula K $: K x y   $- x         $.
  $Regula S $: S x y z $- x z (y z) $.

  $Productum SKK2I $: S K K x $- x $ex
    $Probatione
      $Assumo    S K K x $per $Regula S $.
      $Consequor K x (K x) $ex $Regula S $.
      $Consequor x $ex $Regula K $.
    $.
  $.

$Hypatia Sifr $:
$Lingua A::B $:

  $Alphabetum A B # x y $.
  $Intervalla \sp \th $.
  $Lexicon A# #A B# #B $.
  $Mutabilia x y $.

  $Regula A##A $: x A# #A y $- x       y $.
  $Regula B##B $: x B# #B y $- x       y $.
  $Regula A##B $: x A# #B y $- x #B A# y $.

  $Derivatio Simp $: B# A# #B #A $- $Vacuum $ex
    $Probatione
      $Praemitto B# A# #B #A $.
      $Consequor B# #B A# #A $ex $Regula A##B
                             $cum x $= B# $,
                                  y $= #A $.
      $Consequor       A# #A $ex $Regula B##B
                             $cum x $= $Vacuum $,
                                  y $= A# #A $.
      $Consequor             $ex $Regula A##A
                             $cum x y $= $Vacuum $.
    $.
  $.
